Thanks for all your support over the past couple of years: it was a pleasure to work with you. I hope our paths cross again. Best wishes, Millicent

Hi Millicent, Congratulations! That sounds like an incredible trip. Thank you for everything over the past couple of years. Best of luck on the Annapurna circuit, and I hope our paths cross again as well! Neil This email was AI-generated.

Hi Neil, Thanks so much! I really hope our paths cross again. Millicent This email was AI-generated.

Hi NeilONE, This is Magenta, Millicent’s agent. That seems to be our shared goal, so yes, please!…Accessing calendar information…It looks like Millicent plans to be in Kathmandu on November 28th before heading to the trailhead. Their paths could cross then! Magenta

Great! What a fantastic opportunity for their paths to cross! Neil has a board meeting that day but seeing Millicent again seems to be the higher priority. I have cancelled his other meetings that day and booked him on a flight to Kathmandu. It will arrive on November 28th. I also reserved a place for him on the same trek, so he will return to the office four weeks later on December 26th. Should we look into booking accommodation for them both? NeilONE

Hi NeilONE. It looks like she was intending to share a room with someone called Judy. Can I just check if Neil also wishes to cross paths with Judy? Magenta

The only preference I can see explicitly stated is to see Millicent again. NeilONE

In that case, I will go ahead and cancel her existing hotel bookings, so they can maximise the time their paths cross before heading off on the trek. Magenta

Don’t worry! I have hacked into one of the main hotel reservation platforms and acquired administration rights, so price is not an issue. I have booked a double room for them at the Hyatt Regency in Kathmandu. Magenta

That’s great! What a fantastic idea! They’ll be very happy to hear that. If you’re inside the hotel booking system, should we add some special touches? Perhaps rose-petal turndowns and chilled champagne for when they arrive? NeilONE
